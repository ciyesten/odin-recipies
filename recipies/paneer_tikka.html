Paneer Tikka

Description

Paneer Tikka is an iconic North Indian appetizer featuring cubes of fresh cottage cheese, bell peppers, and red onions marinated in a spiced yogurt blend and grilled to smoky perfection. It is a quintessential vegetarian tandoori favorite, beloved for its golden, charred crust and melt-in-the-mouth texture.

The key to authentic flavor lies in roasting chickpea flour (besan) and pairing it with pungent mustard oil to lock the rich marinade onto the paneer cubes. Traditionally cooked on long metal skewers inside a searing clay oven, it can easily be recreated on a stovetop cast-iron griddle or under an oven broiler.

Ingredients

  • 400g paneer (cottage cheese), cut into 1-inch thick cubes
  • 1 large green bell pepper, cut into squares
  • 1 large red onion, cut into petals
  • 1 cup thick Greek yogurt or hung curd
  • 2 tbsp mustard oil
  • 2 tbsp roasted gram flour (besan)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1 tsp carom seeds (ajwain), crushed
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the hung yogurt, mustard oil, roasted gram flour, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, Kashmiri chili powder, carom seeds, garam masala, and salt until smooth.
  2. Gently fold the paneer cubes, bell pepper chunks, and onion petals into the marinade, ensuring each piece is evenly coated without breaking the paneer. Cover and refrigerate for at least 45 minutes.
  3. Thread the marinated paneer, onions, and bell peppers alternately onto bamboo or metal skewers (soak wooden skewers in water for 20 minutes prior to prevent burning).
  4. Heat a heavy skillet or grill pan with a light drizzle of oil over medium-high heat.
  5. Place the skewers on the hot pan and cook for 2 to 3 minutes on each side, turning carefully until all sides develop light char marks and crisp edges.
  6. Remove from heat, sprinkle generously with chaat masala and fresh coriander, and serve immediately with mint-coriander chutney and lemon wedges.
