Dal Makhani

Description

Dal Makhani is a classic Punjabi lentil preparation made with whole black urad dal and kidney beans, slow-cooked with tomatoes, butter, and fragrant whole spices. Renowned for its rich, earthy aroma and luxurious texture, it remains a centerpiece of celebratory Indian dining.

The signature creaminess does not just come from dairy, but from hours of patient simmering that naturally breaks down the lentil starch into a thick, cohesive gravy. Finished with a touch of crushed fenugreek leaves and a generous pat of butter, it is best enjoyed alongside hot naan or steamed jeera rice.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole black urad dal (black gram), washed and soaked overnight
  • 1/4 cup rajma (kidney beans), washed and soaked overnight
  • 4 cups water for boiling
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp ghee
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1.5 cups fresh tomato puree
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/4 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tsp crushed kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Rinse the soaked urad dal and rajma thoroughly. Pressure cook with 4 cups of water and 1 teaspoon of salt for 6 to 7 whistles (or simmer in a heavy-bottomed pot for 45 minutes) until the lentils are completely tender and crush easily between your fingers.
  2. Lightly mash a portion of the cooked lentils against the side of the pot with the back of a ladle to help thicken the base.
  3. In a deep heavy pan, heat 1 tbsp of ghee and 1 tbsp of butter over medium heat. Add the chopped onions and sauté until golden brown, then stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add the fresh tomato puree, Kashmiri red chili powder, and salt. Cook the masala mixture for 7 to 8 minutes until the fat begins to release from the edges.
  5. Pour the boiled lentils and their remaining cooking water into the masala. Stir well and bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low and let it simmer uncovered for 30 to 40 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  6. Stir in the heavy cream, remaining 2 tbsp of butter, garam masala, and kasuri methi. Simmer for an additional 5 minutes, adjust salt, and serve hot.
