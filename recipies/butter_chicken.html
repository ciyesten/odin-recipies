Butter Chicken

Description

Butter Chicken is one of the most celebrated North Indian curries, known for its tender, spice-marinated chicken simmered in an aromatic, velvety tomato-and-butter gravy. Originating in Delhi, this iconic dish balances subtle heat with mild sweetness, deep tanginess from ripe tomatoes, and rich creaminess.

The magic lies in cooking marinated charred chicken separately before gently finishing it in the silky sauce. Garnished with a drizzle of fresh cream and crushed kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves), it pairs perfectly with hot garlic naan, tandoori roti, or steamed basmati rice.

Ingredients

  • 500g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 cup plain yogurt (curd)
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 3 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp vegetable or mustard oil
  • 2 cups tomato puree (from fresh ripe tomatoes)
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream
  • 1 tbsp crushed kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • 1 tbsp sugar or honey (to balance acidity)
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. In a large bowl, whisk together the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, lemon juice, Kashmiri red chili powder, cumin, garam masala, and salt. Add the chicken pieces and coat thoroughly. Cover and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp of oil in a wide skillet or pan over medium-high heat. Add the marinated chicken in a single layer and sear for 6 to 8 minutes until lightly browned with slight char marks. Remove the chicken and set aside.
  3. In the same pan, melt 2 tbsp of butter over medium heat. Pour in the tomato puree, a pinch of salt, and a dash of red chili powder. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes until the oil starts separating from the sauce.
  4. Stir in the heavy cream and sugar, lowering the heat to maintain a gentle simmer.
  5. Add the cooked chicken back into the gravy. Simmer uncovered for 6 to 8 minutes until the chicken is tender and cooked through.
  6. Stir in the remaining 1 tbsp of butter and crushed kasuri methi. Remove from heat, let it rest for 2 minutes, and serve hot.
